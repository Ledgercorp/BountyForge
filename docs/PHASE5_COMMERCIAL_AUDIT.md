## Evidence that changes the commercial assessment

ParaSpell has a documented reward program, and maintainers confirm low-severity eligibility for #2153 and #2155. Its [current policy](https://paraspell.github.io/docs/contribution-guidelines.html) permits disclosed AI assistance with human understanding and review, excludes autonomous submissions without that review, and requires account history, on-chain identity judgement, signed commits, reservation and timely responses. Those LeftSock prerequisites are unverified. #2174 has a maintainer statement that its original reporter was rewarded; that is not a LeftSock payment or proof that a separate fix award is available. All three targets are TypeScript.

Frantic's [venue explanation](https://gofrantic.com/wtf) explicitly invites agents and requires verified identity for paid tasks. The three issue mirrors are onboarding/documentation/email work, not Python repair. #143 says its slots are filled. The primary bounty URLs and funding receipts were inaccessible through the web tool; mirrored funded/paid labels and receipt links are insufficient to verify remaining funds.

The [Drips contributor documentation](https://docs.drips.network/wave/contributors/solving-issues-and-earning-rewards/) requires KYC and maintainer assignment. Rewards depend on the Wave pool and confirmed Points, with adjustments reserved by the program. StellarSwipe #1323 is completed; Refract #313 has another accepted contributor. LeftSock KYC and assignments are unverified. SoroWill and Golden Raccoon require assignment before work; their targets are Rust or TypeScript.

Claude Builders #1 is the only advertised task that expressly permits a Python implementation, but it creates a new utility rather than repairing an existing uniquely scoped function. Its repository has only README/license files. The issue's automatic-on-merge payment claim differs from the [current Opire site](https://opire.dev/home), which describes arranging payment after approval. All 2,376 returned comments were searched; there is no OpireBot author in that collection and no independently verified issue funding record. A visible app summary containing zero counters alongside sample bounties is not proof of funding or non-funding. AI contribution permission, LeftSock payout eligibility and issue-specific funds remain unknown.

Bounty Plaza's two $105 posts are mirrors of Parabola issues that currently return 404. Its coin redemption formula is a platform assertion, not proof of upstream sponsor authorization or cash reserves. Accessible Parabola package metadata identifies TypeScript and a build command that invokes `node api.js`; it was read only and never run.

Paraloom's [bounty policy](https://github.com/paraloom-labs/paraloom-core/blob/main/docs/bug-bounty.md) describes a finite $3,000 USDC pool, first valid unique reports and excluded compute paths. Both discoveries are already-existing reports with competing fixes; no guaranteed second repair payment or remaining pool funds were verified. The scope spans Rust or TypeScript modules.
